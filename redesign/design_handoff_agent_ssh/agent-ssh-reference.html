§26c Pipeline: the exposure underline

Boundary ops are ordinary chips. The --warn underline (data-key-exposed) marks agent.unlock and every later chip that consumes the exposed slot — it traces where key material travels. Two recipes, same signature, opposite properties:

The boundary path — no marks, output tile unmasked

input | utf8 | agent.sign "SHA256:Ur1h…" namespace=git | out @sig

The unlock path — key material traced in --warn

agent.unlock AABB… | out @me
input | gpg.sign key=@me | out @sig

Underline on agent.unlock, its out, and the consumer of @me — not on the final out @sig, whose value is just a signature.
